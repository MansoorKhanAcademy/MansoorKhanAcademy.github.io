What you can customize

You can change these fields in the editor:

  • Name or title
  • Week of
  • Goal

  • Colour theme and font (including Urdu and Arabic fonts)

How to make it

  1. Open the editor from the button above. The design loads with sample text.
  2. Type your own text into the fields and choose the options.
  3. Pick a colour theme and a font. The preview updates as you type.
  4. Unlock your license key and download a print-ready PNG or PDF, then print on A4 or Letter paper.

Tip: Write the exercise and sets by reps, for example "Push-ups 3 x 12".

Printing and size

This weekly workout planner is portrait and made for A4 paper. Download the PNG to share on WhatsApp or social media, or the PDF to print at home or at a print shop. For a larger copy, print the file at A3.

Frequently asked questions

Can I plan rest days?

Yes. Write "Rest" in the exercise column for the day.

Can I change the first day of the week?

Yes. Use the "Week starts on" option.

What paper size does it print on?

This design is portrait and is made for A4. It also prints on US Letter with small margins, and the high-resolution file can be enlarged to A3.

Is it free to use?
