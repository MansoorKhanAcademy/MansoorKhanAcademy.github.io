What you can customize

You can change these fields in the editor:

  • Your name

How to make it

  1. Open the editor from the button above. The design loads with sample text.
  2. Type your own text into the fields.
  3. Pick a colour theme and a font. The preview updates as you type.
  4. Unlock your license key and download a print-ready PNG or PDF, then print on A4 or Letter paper.

Tip: Colour one box a day at the same time every evening.

Printing and size

This mood tracker is landscape and made for A4 paper. Download the PNG to share on WhatsApp or social media, or the PDF to print at home or at a print shop. For a larger copy, print the file at A3.

Frequently asked questions

Is it a therapy tool?

No. It is a self-reflection sheet. If you feel unwell for a long time, talk to a qualified professional.

Which moods are included?

Happy, Calm, Tired, Anxious, Sad and Angry.

What paper size does it print on?

This design is landscape and is made for A4. It also prints on US Letter with small margins, and the high-resolution file can be enlarged to A3.
