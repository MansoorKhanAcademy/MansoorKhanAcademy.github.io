Calendars and planners printables you can personalize

Calendars and planners you can personalize with a name, month, year and photo, then print on A4 or A3. Choose a monthly calendar, a yearly wall calendar, weekly planners, a meal planner, habit and prayer trackers or a study timetable.

A good calendar or planner only works if it fits your life, so these designs let you change the title, dates and week start before you print. Start the week on Monday or Sunday, add a family photo to the monthly calendar, or build a Ramadan tracker with your own goals. Every design is made for A4 paper and stays sharp when you enlarge it.

Made for: Families, students, teachers and small businesses who want a calendar or planner with their own name and dates.

Frequently asked questions

Can I make a calendar for any year?

Yes. Type any year or month and the weekdays and the number of days are calculated for you, including leap years.

Which planners can I start on Sunday?

The monthly calendar, yearly calendar, weekly planner and weekly meal planner each have a week-start option.
