What you can customize

You can change these fields in the editor:

How to make it

  1. Open the editor from the button above. The design loads with sample text.
  2. Type your own text into the fields, add a photo.
  3. Pick a colour theme and a font. The preview updates as you type.
  4. Unlock your license key and download a print-ready PNG or PDF, then print on A4 or Letter paper.

Tip: Use a portrait photo with the face near the centre because the frame crops to fit.

Printing and size

This photo frame is portrait and made for A4 paper. Download the PNG to share on WhatsApp or social media, or the PDF to print at home or at a print shop. For a larger copy, print the file at A3.

Frequently asked questions

What photo format works?

JPG, PNG and WebP photos work. Large photos are resized automatically so the page stays sharp.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. The photo stays in your browser while you design. It is not uploaded by the editor.

What paper size does it print on?

This design is portrait and is made for A4. It also prints on US Letter with small margins, and the high-resolution file can be enlarged to A3.
