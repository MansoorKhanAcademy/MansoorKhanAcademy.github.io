Frequently asked questions

How does the 52-week challenge work?

You save a growing amount each week. With a step of 100, week 1 is 100, week 2 is 200 and so on.

How much will I save in total?

With step S the total is S multiplied by 1,378, which is the sum of 1 to 52. A step of 100 saves 137,800.

What paper size does it print on?

This design is portrait and is made for A4. It also prints on US Letter with small margins, and the high-resolution file can be enlarged to A3.
