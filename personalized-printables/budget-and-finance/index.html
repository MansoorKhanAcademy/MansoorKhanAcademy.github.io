Budget and finance printables you can personalize

Budget and money printables: a monthly budget planner, expense tracker, 52-week savings challenge, bill payment tracker and debt payoff tracker, with your own currency.

Money is easier to manage when you can see it on paper. These sheets help you plan a month, record spending, save a little each week and pay down debt. You can set the currency to Rs, $, AED or any symbol before printing.

Made for: Households, students, freelancers and small shop owners who want simple paper money tools.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my own currency?

Yes. The budget planner, expense tracker and savings challenge let you type any currency symbol.

Do these sheets calculate totals?

The savings challenge shows the weekly amounts and the final total. The other sheets are for filling by hand.

Is it free to use?

You can open the editor, change the text and preview the design for free. The preview carries a watermark. To download a clean, print-ready PNG or PDF you need a license key from Gumroad, one key for all templates in the studio.
