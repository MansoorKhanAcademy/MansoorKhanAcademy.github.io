How to make it

  1. Open the editor from the button above. The design loads with sample text.
  2. Type your own text into the fields and choose the options.
  3. Pick a colour theme and a font. The preview updates as you type.
  4. Unlock your license key and download a print-ready PNG or PDF, then print on A4 or Letter paper.

Tip: Limit the routine to five or six steps so a child can follow it.

Printing and size

This daily routine chart is portrait and made for A4 paper. Download the PNG to share on WhatsApp or social media, or the PDF to print at home or at a print shop. For a larger copy, print the file at A3.
