Frequently asked questions

How is a behaviour chart different from a reward chart?

A behaviour chart follows daily conduct in a week, while a reward chart counts progress toward one goal.

Can a teacher use it?

Yes. Teachers can put a student's name and week at the top and print one per student.

What paper size does it print on?

This design is portrait and is made for A4. It also prints on US Letter with small margins, and the high-resolution file can be enlarged to A3.
