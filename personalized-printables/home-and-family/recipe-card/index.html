How to make it

This recipe card is landscape and made for A4 paper. Download the PNG to share on WhatsApp or social media, or the PDF to print at home or at a print shop. For a larger copy, print the file at A3.

Frequently asked questions

How do I write the recipe?

Use # Ingredients and # Method headings, then one line per item or step.

Can I print it on A5 cards?

Print on A4 and cut or fold to get A5-sized cards.

What paper size does it print on?

This design is landscape and is made for A4. It also prints on US Letter with small margins, and the high-resolution file can be enlarged to A3.
