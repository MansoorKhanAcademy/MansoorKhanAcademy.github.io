For privacy questions, email mgraphics059@gmail.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy applies to the Tip Calculator Android app. It explains what information is involved when you use the app, how it is used, and what choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

3. Information we collect

We do not run user accounts and we do not ask you to enter personal information. However, when the app is used, some information is handled automatically, mostly by the third-party services described in section 6.

3.1 Technical information sent automatically

  • IP address and basic connection data. Any app that loads content from the internet reveals the IP address of the device to the server it contacts. This happens when the app downloads settings and content.

4. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, and the app does not request permission to access, your name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, calendar, photos, videos, microphone, camera, files, messages, health information or precise location. We do not sell personal information.

5. Information stored on your device

The app saves some information locally on your device so that it works faster and without a connection:

  • Your last bill amount, currency symbol, tip percentage, number of people and rounding preference, so the calculator is ready when you open it again. This is saved only on your device.
  • Cached settings downloaded from GitHub Pages, such as ad and update settings and the "More apps" list.

This information stays on your device. We do not receive it. You can remove it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your phone settings or by uninstalling the app.

6. Third-party services

The app uses the following services. They have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to read.

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). The app downloads settings (such as ad and update settings) from files hosted on GitHub Pages. The app only reads from them. GitHub may log the IP address of the request. GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Unity Ads. Used to show advertising. Unity Privacy Policy and Unity's Google Play data disclosure.

7. Advertising and consent

Tip Calculator may show advertising provided by Unity Ads. Ads can be switched on or off by us from our server. When ads are on, the ad service may use your advertising ID to show and measure ads.

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings, usually under Settings, Privacy, Ads.
  • In regions where the law requires your consent before ads are personalised or before advertising data is collected, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we will not show ads unless a consent request is shown and accepted. Until then, the app does not show ads in those regions.
  • Ads are not intended to appear while you are using the main features of the app in a way that blocks the content for long periods. We limit how often full-screen ads appear.

8. How we use information

  • To provide the app and deliver content, settings and updates.
  • To show and measure advertising through our ad provider.
  • To keep the app secure, prevent fraud and fix technical problems.
  • To follow the law and the rules of app stores and ad networks.

Where privacy law such as the EU and UK GDPR applies, our legal bases are: providing the app you asked to use (contract), our legitimate interest in running and securing the app, and your consent for advertising where consent is required.

9. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers listed in section 6, who process it for the purposes described, and where required by law or to protect our rights, for example to respond to a lawful request.

10. Data retention

We do not keep personal information about you on our own servers. Data held by third-party services is kept under their policies. Data stored on your device remains until you clear it or uninstall the app.

11. Security

The app connects to our servers and to third-party services over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal information about you, to object to certain processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To use these rights, contact us at mgraphics059@gmail.com. You can also:

  • reset or opt out of the advertising ID in your Android settings;
  • clear locally stored data in your phone settings;
  • uninstall the app at any time.

13. Requesting deletion

Because the app has no accounts, we do not hold a user profile that we can look up. If you believe data about you has been collected through the app and want it deleted, email mgraphics059@gmail.com with the subject "Tip Calculator data request". We will reply within 30 days. For data held by Unity, you can also use the privacy request options in Unity's privacy policy.

14. Children

Tip Calculator is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help to have it removed.

15. International transfers

We are based in Pakistan, and the third-party services we use may process data in other countries, including the United States. By using the app you understand that information may be transferred to and processed in countries whose data protection laws may differ from those of your own country.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Please review it regularly. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
