What this app does (English)

Invoice Stock Khata helps a shopkeeper keep the whole business in one place. No login, no internet needed — your data stays on your phone.

Stock Management

See how much stock you have. Add new stock, remove damaged stock, and get a reminder when stock is low.

Create Bills / Invoices

Make a clean bill in a few taps. Add items, rate, quantity and discount. Save the bill as a picture and share it on WhatsApp.

Khata / Credit Account Management

Keep a separate khata for every customer. See the full history of bills and payments in one place.

Manage Udhaar, Baqaya Jaat and Recovery / Vasuli

See who owes how much. Record the money you receive and check the remaining balance any time.

Customer Records

Save each customer's name, phone number, address and area. Find any customer quickly.

Easy for shopkeepers and small businesses

Simple buttons, works offline, and supports both English and Urdu. Made for daily shop work.
