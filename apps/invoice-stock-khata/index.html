How to get the app

This app is in Testing Phase (Early Access) on Google Play. It is not open to everyone yet. Follow 3 easy steps.

  1. 1

    Join the Tester Group

    Open the group link and press Join group. Use the same Google account that you use on Google Play.

  2. 2

    Wait a little

    Access can take a few minutes or a few hours to become active.

  3. 3

    Install from Play Store

    Open the Play Store link and press Install.

Important: if you do not join the tester group, the app will not install from the Play Store.

What the app does

The whole shop in one place. No login and no internet needed. Your data stays on your phone.

📦

Stock

See how much stock you have. Add new stock, remove damaged stock, and get a reminder when stock is low.

🧾

Bills / Invoices

Make a clean bill in a few taps with items, rate, quantity and discount. Save it as a picture and share on WhatsApp.

📒

Customer Khata

A separate khata for every customer, with the full history of bills and payments.

💰

Udhaar, Baqaya and Vasuli

See who owes how much. Record money received and check the balance any time.

👥

Customer Records

Save name, phone number, address and area. Find any customer quickly.

📴

Easy and offline

Simple buttons, works without internet, in English and Urdu. Made for daily shop work.
