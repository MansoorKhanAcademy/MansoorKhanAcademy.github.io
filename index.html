What is Mansoor Khan Academy?

Mansoor Khan Academy is an independent developer brand from Pakistan that builds free Android apps and tools for content creators.

What is Prompt Book: AI Video Prompts?

A free Android app with ready-to-use prompts for AI video and image generators. You pick a niche, copy a prompt, paste it into your AI tool and create content for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Facebook Reels and Instagram Reels. The app does not generate videos itself.

How can I install it?

It is in Google Play early access. Join the testers group, wait a short time, then open the Google Play page and tap Install.

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free to use and contains ads.
