Umrah Preparation Checklist: What to Prepare Before You Travel

By Mansoor Khan, Mansoor Khan Academy · 2026-11-19

Preparing for Umrah is easier when everything is written down. Rules for visas, vaccinations and travel change from time to time, so always check the latest requirements from official sources and your travel agent. The lists below are a personal planning aid, and they do not replace official guidance.

Documents to prepare

Passport with enough validity, visa, flight tickets, hotel bookings, copies of IDs, passport photos and any health papers required at the time of travel. Keep one copy in your hand luggage and one in your suitcase. The Umrah checklist groups these items so you can tick them off.

Clothing

Men prepare ihram clothing and a belt bag. Women prepare modest, comfortable clothing. Everyone needs comfortable footwear, since there is a lot of walking. Choose light cotton for warm weather.

Health and comfort

Bring personal medicines in original packaging, simple first aid items, unscented soap, a water bottle and a small towel. If you take regular medicines, ask your doctor about carrying them.

Worship items

A small dua book, a tasbeeh and a prayer mat are common choices. Many pilgrims also write down duas they want to make, so they have them ready.

Electronics

Phone, charger, power bank and a travel adapter. Download offline maps and keep important contact numbers on paper in case your phone battery is low.

Plan your days

Write a simple plan for each day, including travel, hotel check-in and rest. A trip itinerary page helps you keep flight times and bookings in one place. Leave free time for rest.

Pack in an organised way

Use a packing list and pack in small bags by category, such as documents, clothes and toiletries. Weigh your suitcase before you leave to avoid extra charges.

Quick tips

  • Start preparing at least a few weeks before travel.
  • Check airline baggage limits and keep a small bag for essentials.
  • Share a copy of your itinerary with a family member at home.
  • Ask your travel agent or the official authorities for the latest rules.
