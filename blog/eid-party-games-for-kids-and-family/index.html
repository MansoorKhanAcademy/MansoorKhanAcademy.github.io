Eid Party Games for Kids and Family: Easy Printable Ideas

By Mansoor Khan, Mansoor Khan Academy · 2026-11-19

On Eid, relatives and neighbours visit, and the children often get restless once the food is finished. A few simple printed games keep everyone busy and bring different generations together. Here are ideas you can prepare the night before.

Eid guessing game with yes or no questions

Choose a secret person from the family. The others ask only yes or no questions to find out who it is. The question cards have an Eid and Ramadan theme with questions such as "Does your person wear a kurta?" or "Did your person get Eidi today?".

Who am I? with family clues

Write three clues for each relative, for example "I love sewayyan" or "I always make the best tea". Use Who am I cards and let the children read the clues aloud.

Baby photo quiz

Collect childhood photos and print a baby photo card for each one. Guests write their guesses and the person with the most correct answers wins. Always ask the people in the photos first.

Eidi treasure hunt

Hide small envelopes or sweets around the house and write simple clues on paper. Children follow the clues to find their Eidi. Older children can help write the clues for the younger ones.

Team competition

Divide guests into teams of adults and children together. Play a few rounds and write the points on a score sheet. Mixed teams stop the game from being too easy for the adults.

Quick tips

  • Prepare the games the night before so Eid day is relaxed.
  • Keep the prizes small, such as chocolates or stickers.
  • Have a quiet game ready for guests who prefer not to run around.
  • Print two copies of every card in case one gets lost.
